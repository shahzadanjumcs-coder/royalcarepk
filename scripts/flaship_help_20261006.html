Step-by-Step

HOW FLASHIP WORKS

Everything you need to ship, track, and collect — in three simple steps.

01
Step 1
Create Your Account
Sign up free at partners.flaship.pk. Add your store details, CNIC, and bank account for COD withdrawals. Approval is instant for most sellers.
  • No registration fee
  • Verified within minutes
  • Connect Shopify, WooCommerce, or use our REST API
Get started →
02
Step 2
Book & Ship
Enter order details, compare courier rates, and book in seconds. Print your shipping label and hand the parcel to the courier at your doorstep.
  • Single or bulk CSV booking
  • A6 & thermal label formats
  • Courier pickup from your location
03
Step 3
Track & Get Paid
Monitor every shipment in real-time from your dashboard. COD is credited to your Flaship Wallet on delivery. Withdraw to any bank account, 24/7.
  • Live tracking across all couriers
  • Instant wallet credit on delivery
  • No withdrawal fees, ever
